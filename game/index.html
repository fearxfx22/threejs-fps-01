DUSTLINE

A round-based tactical FPS in a desert-dusk arena. Two teams of four, first to 5 rounds. Plant the bomb on site A or B — or wipe the other team out. Earn cash, buy better guns, and stop to aim: this game rewards a steady hand.

  • E (hold) Plant / defuse the bomb   Esc Pause
